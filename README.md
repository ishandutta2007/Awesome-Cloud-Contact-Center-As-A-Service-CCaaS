## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms 💼

> **Market Insights & Industry Overview**: The global Cloud Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) market is estimated at **$40.35 Billion – $50.82 Billion (2026)** with an 18%–25% CAGR. The sector is **moderately to highly fragmented**—competed heavily by cloud giants (AWS), enterprise communication veterans (Cisco, NICE, Genesys), and specialized AI-native CCaaS vendors.

The CCaaS market has traditionally relied on **per-seat per-month pricing** that scales linearly with agent headcount. A 500-agent Genesys deployment costs **$85,000–$115,000 per month**, while an equivalent VICIdial cluster runs **$18,000–$35,000**—a nearly **$1 million annual difference**. **Amazon Connect** and **Twilio Flex** pioneered **pay-per-use and per-active-hour pricing** with zero per-seat commitment, while **Genesys Cloud CX** leads in enterprise **omnichannel AI routing**.

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects ⚡

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/chatwoot/chatwoot?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot/stargazers)  
  **Open-source customer engagement suite & omnichannel contact center**, MIT licensed. **Alternative to Intercom/Zendesk** with live chat, email, WhatsApp, Line, and webhooks. Integrated agent desktop, omnichannel inbox, and conversation routing. **Over 22k+ stars** makes it the most popular open-source support workspace. 💬

- **[LiveKit Agents](https://github.com/livekit/agents)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/livekit/agents?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/livekit/agents/stargazers)  
  **Real-time voice & multimodal AI agent framework**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Sub-second latency voice pipelines** for contact centers and voice assistants. Integrates seamlessly with SIP ingress/egress, WebRTC, turn-detection, OpenAI, Deepgram, and ElevenLabs. 🎙️

- **[Asterisk](https://github.com/asterisk/asterisk)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/asterisk/asterisk?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/asterisk/asterisk/stargazers)  
  **The open-source telephony engine that powers most contact center platforms**, GPL-2.0 licensed. **Not call center software** — it is a **PBX framework**, a programmable phone system. **Handles SIP connectivity, call routing, codec transcoding, and VoIP protocol management**. The foundational raw material for VICIdial, OMniLeads, and Wazo. 🏗️

- **[Pipecat](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/pipecat-ai/pipecat?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/stargazers)  
  **Open-source framework for voice and multimodal AI agents**, BSD-2-Clause licensed. Built for ultra-low latency real-time voice contact center bots. Handles STT -> LLM -> TTS pipelines, audio interruption, and telephony integrations (Twilio, Daily, SIP). 🐱

- **[FreeSWITCH](https://github.com/signalwire/freeswitch)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/signalwire/freeswitch?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/signalwire/freeswitch/stargazers)  
  **Scalable open-source cross-platform telephony platform**, MPL-2.0 licensed. **Alternative to Asterisk** — powers **ai-native-callcenter** and **FusionPBX**. **Highly scalable** for high-concurrency voice environments and modern AI call center stacks. ⚡

- **[Bolna](https://github.com/bolna-ai/bolna)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bolna-ai/bolna?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/bolna-ai/bolna/stargazers)  
  **Open-source framework for building ultra-fast AI voice agents**, Apache-2.0 licensed. Designed for automated conversational call center agents, outbound phone campaigns, and real-time customer support with sub-second response times. ⚡

- **[ai-native-callcenter](https://github.com/rasonyang/ai-native-callcenter)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/rasonyang/ai-native-callcenter?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/rasonyang/ai-native-callcenter/stargazers)  
  **Self-hosted, API-first AI call center**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **The AI is the default answer, not an add-on**. **One Go binary** — REST API, SSE event stream, and web interface inside it, plus PostgreSQL and FreeSWITCH. Speech-to-speech voice model answers calls and transfers to human agent queues in-browser. 🤖

- **[VICIdial](https://github.com/ricklabe/VICIdial)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ricklabe/VICIdial?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ricklabe/VICIdial/stargazers)  
  **The dominant open-source contact center platform**, AGPL-2.0 licensed. **Over 14,000 installations worldwide**. **Battle-tested predictive dialer** — true predictive, progressive, ratio, power, and manual dialing modes. Inbound ACD, blended campaigns, call recording, real-time monitoring, agent scripting, and non-agent API. 🏆

- **[OMniLeads](https://github.com/alejandrozf/ominicontacto)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/alejandrozf/ominicontacto?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/alejandrozf/ominicontacto/stargazers)  
  **100% open-source WebRTC contact center software**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Inbound, preview, and manual outbound campaigns** natively; predictive dialer via integration APIs. WebRTC agent & supervisor console, answering machine detection, recording, and CRM integration. 🏛️

- **[unpod](https://github.com/unpod-ai/unpod)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/unpod-ai/unpod?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/unpod-ai/unpod/stargazers)  
  **Open-Source CPaaS for Contact Center Teams**, open-source. Full-stack monorepo (Next.js 16, Django 5, FastAPI, LiveKit, Pipecat). Multi-tenant organization with RBAC, voice agent studio, knowledge base, and desktop client. 🏗️

- **[Dograh](https://github.com/dograh-hq/dograh)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/dograh-hq/dograh?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/dograh-hq/dograh/stargazers)  
  **Self-hosted voice AI call center platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed. Visual workflow builder to design, test, and deploy AI voice agents for inbound support and automated outbound calling. 🐶

- **[Wazo Platform](https://github.com/wazo-platform/wazo-docker)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/wazo-platform/wazo-docker?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/wazo-platform/wazo-docker/stargazers)  
  **Full-featured IPBX solution built atop Asterisk**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Modern APIs and microservices architecture**. Contact center module handles inbound queues and basic agent management. 🎯

- **[Tring](https://github.com/anzalabidi/tring)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/anzalabidi/tring?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/anzalabidi/tring/stargazers)  
  **Open-source voice agent stack**, MIT licensed. Local-first STT/LLM/TTS pipeline (faster-whisper, Ollama, Kokoro) with honest cost accounting per minute and per conversation. 🎙️

- **[ZeroVoice](https://github.com/collinparan/zero-voice)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/collinparan/zero-voice?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/collinparan/zero-voice/stargazers)

## ⚠️ Disclaimer ℹ️

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Open-source contact center software is not free** — VICIdial itself is free, but at 100 agents the real monthly costs are **$5,600–$14,800** including server infrastructure, SIP trunking, DIDs, system administration, STIR/SHAKEN compliance, and monitoring. **The per-agent cost drops dramatically at scale** — at 500 agents, VICIdial runs **$25–$60/agent/month** vs Genesys at flat or increasing rates.
- **VICIdial wins on outbound predictive dialing** — its adaptive algorithm and granular campaign configuration are unmatched. **Genesys wins on inbound/omnichannel** — true unified routing across voice, email, chat, SMS, and social.
- **Asterisk is not call center software** — it is a PBX framework. You must build contact center workflows on top of it.
- Open-source solutions (VICIdial, OMniLeads, ai-native-callcenter) provide self-hosted ownership, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees and 24/7 support remain primarily commercial offerings. 📞
